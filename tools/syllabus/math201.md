# Math 201 syllabus (Term 261)

Textbook: Larson & Edwards, *Calculus: Early Transcendental Functions, Metric Version*, 7th ed.
Course: Calculus III (3-0-3). Final exam is MCQ and comprehensive.

| Sec | Topic |
|---|---|
| 10.2 | Plane curves and parametric equations (eliminating the parameter, orientation, restricted parameter ranges) |
| 10.3 | Parametric equations and calculus (slope, tangent lines, arc length, area under a parametric curve, surface area of revolution) |
| 10.4 | Polar coordinates and polar graphs (conversions, symmetry, tangent lines to polar curves, recognizing curves such as roses, limacons, cardioids) |
| 10.5 | Area and arc length in polar coordinates (including intersections of polar graphs) |
| 11.1 | Vectors in the plane |
| 11.2 | Space coordinates and vectors in space |
| 11.3 | The dot product of two vectors (angle, projection, direction cosines) |
| 11.4 | The cross product of two vectors in space (area, volume of parallelepiped, triple product) |
| 11.5 | Lines and planes in space, including skew lines and distances |
| 11.6 | Surfaces in space (cylinders and quadric surfaces; surfaces of revolution is omitted) |
| 13.1 | Introduction to functions of several variables (domain, range, level curves and level surfaces) |
| 13.2 | Limits and continuity (excluding the epsilon-delta definition) |
| 13.3 | Partial derivatives (including higher-order) |
| 13.4 | Differentials (total differential, linear approximation) |
| 13.5 | Chain rules for functions of several variables (implicit differentiation) |
| 13.6 | Directional derivatives and gradients |
| 13.7 | Tangent planes and normal lines |
| 13.8 | Extrema of functions of two variables (critical points, second partials test, absolute extrema on closed regions) |
| 13.9 | Applications of extrema (only optimization problems) |
| 13.10 | Lagrange multipliers |
| 14.1 | Iterated integrals and area in the plane |
| 14.2 | Double integrals and volume (change of order of integration) |
| 14.3 | Change of variables: polar coordinates (double integrals in polar form) |
| 14.6 | Triple integrals and applications |
| 11.7 | Cylindrical and spherical coordinates |
| 14.7 | Triple integrals in other coordinates (cylindrical and spherical) |

Typical coverage: Exam I = 10.2 to 11.4, Exam II = 11.5 to 13.6, Final = comprehensive (all sections above).
Sections 13.7 to 13.10 and Chapter 14 (with 11.7) appear only on the Final.
Older terms may have covered slightly different sections. Keep those questions and solve them with the
textbook method for that section.

Explicitly omitted in the term 261 syllabus: surfaces of revolution (11.6), the epsilon-delta definition of a limit
(13.2), applications of extrema other than optimization problems (13.9).
Not in the term 261 schedule (so not examined): 14.4, 14.5 (mass, surface area), Chapter 12, and Chapter 15
(line integrals, Green, Stokes, divergence).

Prerequisites (allowed freely): Math 101/102 calculus (all integration techniques), high-school algebra and
trigonometry.
