# Math 101 syllabus (Term 261)

Source: https://math.kfupm.edu.sa/docs/default-source/css-library/math101-261.pdf (saved as syl261.pdf).
Parts marked "STATED" come from that syllabus. Parts marked "INFERRED" are read off the old exams (terms 221 to 251)
and are not in the syllabus.

Textbook (STATED): Larson & Edwards, *Calculus: Early Transcendental Functions, Metric Version*, 7th ed.
Course (STATED): Calculus I (4-0-4). Prerequisite: one-year preparatory mathematics or equivalent.
Exam I = 14 MCQ (90 min), Exam II = 14 MCQ (90 min), Final = 20 MCQ (120 min) and comprehensive.
Description (STATED): limits, continuity, differentiation, techniques of differentiation, implicit differentiation, extrema,
first and second derivative tests, concavity, inflection points, curve sketching, applied extrema, Mean Value Theorem.

## Coverage per exam (STATED, term 261)

- Exam I (Sep 28): 2.1 to 3.2 and 4.5 (limits, continuity, limits at infinity, derivative definition, tangent lines, basic rules).
- Exam II (Nov 2): 3.3 to 4.2 (product/quotient/chain rules, implicit differentiation, inverse functions, related rates, Newton's
  method, extrema on an interval, Rolle and MVT).
- Final: comprehensive, 20 MCQ (so also 4.3, 4.4, 5.6, 4.6, 4.7, 4.8, 5.1, 5.9 up to Example 3).

## Explicitly omitted or limited (STATED)

- Section 2.2: only up to page 75; the formal (epsilon-delta) definition of a limit is excluded.
- Section 5.9: only up to Example 3.
- Section 3.5: normal lines are covered through Exercises 63-64.
- Sections not in the pacing schedule are not taught (for example Chapter 1, 5.2 to 5.5, 5.7, 5.8, and Chapter 6 onward).

## Observations from the old exams (INFERRED, not from the syllabus)

- Earlier terms (221 to 251) used 14 or 18 questions for Exam I and II and 20 or 28 for the Final (see the report table).
  Terms 241, 243 and 233 follow the current 14/14/20 pattern; 221 to 223 and 231, 232 use 18/18/28.
- Older Exam I papers (221, 222, 232, 231) include epsilon-delta questions of the type "largest delta such that |f(x) - L| < eps"
  (221 e1 Q2, 222 e1 Q8, 221 fx Q5). The term 261 syllabus excludes this definition, so these questions are probably out of scope
  now. Keep them and solve them with the textbook method.
- Exam II papers in older terms start with Newton's method and 3.3 to 3.8, then 4.1 and 4.2, matching the 3.3-4.2 split.
- Finals add 4.3, 4.4, 4.6 to 4.8, L'Hopital's rule, hyperbolic functions (including identities such as "if sinh x = a, then tanh x")
  and simple indefinite integrals (5.1, for example the integral of (x+6)/sqrt(x), of 2x^3 - sec^2 x, or of sec y (tan y - sec y)).
- Some older finals also ask the derivative of inverse trigonometric functions, logarithmic differentiation and the limit
  forms 1^infinity and 0^0, which belong to 3.4 and 5.6.
- Printed graph-reading questions are rare in this course (only one found in terms 221 to 251: 242 e1 Q1).

Prerequisites (allowed freely, INFERRED from the course description and exams): high-school algebra, trigonometry,
exponential and logarithmic functions, inverse trigonometric functions and hyperbolic function definitions.
